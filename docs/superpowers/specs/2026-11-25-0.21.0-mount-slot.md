# 0.21.0: Quick slots, first slot: the mount

Status: built on 2026-11-25 as 0.21.0 (see "As built" at the end), not yet
tested on a device. The text below is the spec as it was written before the
build ("draft, waiting for the owner's yes"); only this line and "As built"
were added.

## Why

The owner is level 41. On this client a mount cannot be bound to a controller
button from the Collections menu; the advice is to drag it onto a spell bar,
which is hard on a handheld and costs a spell slot. The owner (2026-11-25):
"idk how im gonna play past 40 without a mount to easily jump on". They do not
want a mount put on anyone's bars for them.

## What the player gets

One square with the mount's picture inside the window the PE button opens
(the chat window), on a "Quick slots" line under the chat buttons. Tap PE,
tap the square: mounted, and the window closes. The same two taps get you
off. It is the first of the **Quick slots** (the 2026-11-21 idea: a short row
of player-filled squares at the bottom of that window). Only the mount slot
is built now; hearthstone, food and the like come later, in the same row.

```
  +------------------- Chat --------------------+
  |  [ Say ]  [ Party ]  [ Guild ]  [ ... ]     |
  |  [ whisper names ... ]                      |
  |  Quick slots                                |
  |  [mount]                                    |
  +---------------------------------------------+
```

The owner chose inside the window over a square on the playing screen
(2026-11-25): "in an effort to keep things clean ... lets get things working
first, and then lets add polish".

- **In a fight** the square is greyed and a tap does nothing but say "Not in
  a fight" in the window (you cannot mount in a fight).
- **Mounted:** the square shows a bright edge and its label reads "Get off".
- **Cannot mount here** (indoors, swimming): the picture is greyed. A tap
  still asks the game, which says why in its own red text.
- **No mount owned:** the square shows "?" and its label reads "No mount
  yet".
- **Which mount:** a row "Mount" steps through the mounts this character
  owns. Until the player picks one it reads "First favourite": a mount
  starred in the Collections menu if there is one, else the first mount the
  character can use.
- **Row "Quick slots":** Off / On, Off by default like every untried part.
  Both rows sit with the Chat picker's settings, since the square lives in
  its window. It needs Chat picker On (with it Off a tap on PE opens the
  settings, so there is no window to hold the square; the row says so). No
  reload is needed.
- **Touch or controller:** the square is one more stop in the window's own
  D-pad walk, so PE's window can be driven as it is today.

## Not in this build

- Item or spell slots (they need the game's locked buttons and a probe test).
- A controller button that mounts without opening the window.
- A square on the playing screen, or a piece in Arrange it yourself.
- Empty "+" squares for slots that do nothing yet.
- Anything on the player's spell bars.

## What would make it a failure

- A refused action, or anything that stops spells casting.
- The window or the square changing anything during a fight.
- A tap that does nothing and says nothing.

## Approaches

1. **A plain button of ours calling the game's mount call (chosen).** The
   game's own Mount button in the Collections menu is an ordinary button that
   calls `C_MountJournal.SummonByID(mountID)`
   (`Blizzard_Collections/Classic/Blizzard_MountCollection.lua:498-511`,
   `Mainline/Blizzard_MountCollection.lua:833`); the call is not marked as
   locked in `MountJournalDocumentation.lua:614`. Small, and it touches no spell
   bar.
2. **"Summon a random favourite" (`SummonByID(0)`).** No picker needed, but
   the game's own button for it is commented out in the Classic file
   (`Classic/Blizzard_MountCollection.lua:622-643`), so it may not work on
   this client. Not used; a named mount is the safer call.
3. **Put the mount in a spare spell slot for the player.** Works with the
   controller, but the owner does not want slots assigned for people.
4. **A locked spell-style button casting the mount by name.** The fallback if
   approach 1 is refused on the device. Bigger: such a button cannot be shown, hidden or
   moved in a fight by us.

## Game calls (all to be confirmed by `scripts/audit-api.sh`)

- `C_MountJournal.SummonByID(mountID)`, `C_MountJournal.Dismiss()` on a tap.
- `C_MountJournal.GetMountIDs()`, `C_MountJournal.GetMountInfoByID(id)` for
  the name, picture, favourite, owned, usable and "active" answers.
- `IsMounted()` for the bright edge, `InCombatLockdown()` for the grey-out.
  Both are read when the window is drawn and on a tap; nothing listens while
  the window is closed.

Unverified until the device: that an addon's tap may call `SummonByID` on
this client; what `GetMountInfoByID` answers here for "usable" and
"favourite". Worst case: the tap does nothing, or the game refuses it and the
Errors page shows a blocked `SummonByID`. Either way the row goes back to Off
and nothing else is affected.

## Rules that apply

- Our button is our own frame: no field is written on a game frame, no
  Edit Mode piece is touched.
- Nothing ticks, On or Off: the square is drawn when the window opens.
- Callbacks go through `PE:Safe` / `PE:Wrap`.
- The chosen mount is saved per character (mounts differ by character).

## Left to the builder

- The square's look (edge, grey-out, label), in the style of the window's
  other buttons.
- How the window's height and D-pad walk take in the new line.

## Device test (TESTING section 36, to be written with the build)

Turn the row On. Tap PE: the Quick slots line is under the chat buttons. Tap
the square outdoors: mounted, window closed. Tap PE and the square again:
off. In a fight, open the window: square greyed, a tap says "Not in a fight".
Step the "Mount" row to another mount and try it. Indoors: a tap shows the
game's own message. Reach the square with the D-pad. Check the Errors page.

## As built (2026-11-25, 0.21.0)

Built as approach 1. Files: `addons/PocketEssentials/QuickSlots.lua` (the
rules; no frame, no event, no timer), the line and the two rows in
`Chat.lua`, tests in `tests/features/quickslots_test.lua`. Nothing here has
run in the game.

Game calls, each found in Blizzard's source for the pinned build by
`scripts/audit-api.sh` (all "documented"):

- `C_MountJournal.GetMountIDs` (`MountJournalDocumentation.lua:264`)
- `C_MountJournal.GetMountInfoByID` (`:273`)
- `C_MountJournal.SummonByID` (`:614`), never with id 0
- `C_MountJournal.Dismiss` (`:49`)
- `IsMounted` (`PlayerScriptDocumentation.lua:1399`)
- `InCombatLockdown` was already in use (`PE:InCombat`).

Where the build differs from this spec or the plan, and why:

1. **The two rows are not in the setup guide.** Its "Round button and chat"
   screen already holds the round button's choices and the Chat picker's
   two rows; two more push the last row under the fold at 720p, and the
   part is untried. They are in the settings only. A row can now be marked
   `guide = false` (`Settings.lua`, `Drawer:OptionList`).
2. **"Quick slots" saves nothing until it is tapped.** The plan had
   `opts.quickSlots` 0 as a saved default; an older rule (and its tests)
   says the Chat picker saves nothing but its window size until the player
   changes something, so nothing saved means Off.
3. **The row "Quick slots" is drawn with Chat picker Off too**, where it
   reads "Quick slots: Needs Chat picker" (as the plan says). It can be
   switched On there; nothing is built until Chat picker is On. The row
   "Mount" shows only with Chat picker On, whether Quick slots is On or
   Off.
4. **The "Mount" row always shows its control.** With no mount it reads "No
   mount yet" between two dimmed ends, not a bare row name. Under the
   choice a small line names the mount "First favourite" stands for right
   now, or the mount's place ("2 of 5").
5. **The square's look.** One and a half buttons a side, the picture inside
   the button's edge, the label beside it (not under it), "?" inside it
   with no mount. Mounted: the button's own "selected" look (gold edge).
   Greyed: the button's dim style and a grey picture.
6. **"Not in a fight" is shown in the label's place**, in gold, beside the
   square: the window has no note line of its own. A tap with no mount
   turns "No mount yet" gold, so no tap is silent.
7. **Mounted in a fight, the square reads "Get off" but a tap does
   nothing** except say "Not in a fight". The spec's rule "in a fight, no
   game call at all" was kept over getting off in a fight: the smaller,
   safer choice. The game's own ways off a mount are not touched.
8. **"Mounted" is `IsMounted()` or the picked mount's own answer**, so on a
   mount other than the picked one the square still reads "Get off" and the
   tap gets off.
9. **The square is not redrawn while the window stays open** (nothing
   listens, as the spec asks). A fight that starts with the window open
   does not grey it; the tap is still stopped, because the tap asks again,
   and the window is then redrawn.
10. **A mount the game marks "hide on this character" is left out** of the
    list, like one that is not owned. A mount whose "owned" answer is
    hidden from addons is left out too.
11. **A missing or hidden answer counts as "no"**: not usable (greyed, a tap
    still asks), not a favourite, not active. A hidden name shows as
    "Mount".
12. **A fifth tap answer, "missing"**: the journal lists mounts but has no
    `SummonByID` or `Dismiss`. Nothing is called and the line reads "Not
    available on this version of the game".
13. **`QS:Line()` also returns the list and whether the mount is the
    built-in choice, and `QS:MountLabel()` a second, small line**; the plan
    listed one answer each.
14. **A saved mount that is no longer owned** falls back to "First
    favourite" and stays saved, as the plan says, until the player steps
    the row; a step then counts from "First favourite".

Tests and tools changed besides the new test file: the stand-in client got
a mount journal and `IsMounted` (`tests/lib/fakeclient.lua`, none owned by
default); `tests/features/setup_test.lua`'s row finder no longer takes the
control of the row above for a row with a help line (it did for "Quick
slots", which sits under a row without one); `tests/taint/calls.txt` has
lines for `Next` (ours) and `SetDesaturated` (the engine's texture method
on our own texture).

What the offline tests cannot show (`docs/ARCHITECTURE.md`, "Unverified
API"):

- Whether the game takes `SummonByID` and `Dismiss` from an addon's tap.
- What the journal answers on this client for owned, favourite, usable and
  active, and how long its list is.
- How the square looks at 720p, and whether two taps are quick enough.

## After the review

An independent review found nothing to stop a device test. Changed after it:

- With "Quick slots" Off the "Mount" row reads "Turn Quick slots on" and the
  mount list is not read at all, so Off keeps every untried call asleep.
- A tap in a fight returns before the mount list is read. (The window is
  still drawn again after such a tap, which reads the list once.)
- The line reference for the game's switched-off random-favourite button is
  622-643.
- Known and left: the game's own Mount button plays an "unwrap" animation for
  a mount never opened before; ours goes straight to the summon. A tap acts on
  how things are at the tap, not on what the square showed when the window
  opened.

# 0.19.0: the square map

Status: **approved 2026-11-25** by the owner's "is it possible to do the one build with the map,
and just follow it up with a second build for everything else?", said while away at work
(an away batch: built, reviewed and published as a pre-release with nobody to ask). The look was picked on 2026-11-25 from
the mockup (artboard C, "Square map, bigger": https://claude.ai/artifact/CbBdMJCiFjjxDKzLRpfjNu),
after the first device sitting of 0.18.0. The owner's words: "can we make it look more elvui
ish", "it used to be bigger", then "C for sure, i like the bigger map".

Blizzard's source is `~/.cache/wow-ui-source/forever` at `c01a87b`, build 1.60.1 (71091);
paths below are under `Interface/AddOns/`. `M/` is `Blizzard_Minimap/`. Every game call the
builder adds is verified there first and cited by file and line (CLAUDE.md, ARCHITECTURE
"Calls into Blizzard's Lua").

## Why

The playing screen now has flat, thin-edged bars of our own (0.17.0) and can be arranged
(0.18.0). The map is the piece that still looks like the old screen: round, at 70% (139
across; it started at 80% and lost its gold ring in 0.16.0), with the place name on a
separate strip below it. The owner wants it bigger and in the same flat style as the bars.

## Goals

- A square map, 200 across with its edge, at the bottom middle between the two inner spell
  panels, with a 1-unit black edge like the slim bars.
- The place name, the clock and the coordinates drawn inside the map; no strip underneath.
- Behind one switch, Off by default; Off gives exactly today's screen.
- The owner's saved arrangement keeps working: nothing they moved jumps.

## Not in this version

- A round map at a bigger size, a map in a top corner (the second arrangement), a map
  size row for the square.
- The other items from the 2026-11-25 handoff (the owner's arrangement as the built-in
  one, removing the Part rows, whole names on the bars, the wheel buttons). Each has its
  own spec. The owner has been asked whether they ship in the same build.

## What would make it a failure

- A refused controller action. No call into Blizzard's Lua that writes a field or a global,
  and no field set on a game frame (the 0.16.3 rule).
- A map that is square until the game redraws it, then round with a square edge.
- Quest icons of ours floating outside the map or cut off at a circle inside the square.

## Approaches considered

1. **The game's map, given a square mask (chosen).** `Minimap:SetMaskTexture` is the
   engine's method on the map widget (`Blizzard_APIDocumentationGenerated/MinimapFrameAPIDocumentation.lua:141`);
   the game itself calls it with its round mask in `MinimapClusterMixin:UpdateMinimapConfig`
   (`M/Camelot/Minimap.lua:42`), the function 0.16.0 already hooks to hide the gold ring. We
   give it a plain white square (`Interface\Buttons\WHITE8X8`) and give it again after each
   redraw; Off puts the game's mask back (`ui-hud-minimap-frame-generic-mask`,
   `M/Camelot/Minimap.lua:11`). Size comes from the Edit Mode Size setting as today.
2. **A map frame of our own.** Not possible: an addon cannot create a second map widget
   that shows the ground.
3. **Keep the round map and only make it bigger with a thin edge.** Cheaper and with no
   unknowns, but it is not the look the owner picked. It stays the fallback if the mask
   cannot be changed on the device.

## The design

### The switch

A row on the Controller tab, **Map look**: `Round` (today, the default) / `Square, bigger`.
Stored as `opts.mapLook` (0 / 1) in the layout's options like `mapButtons`. Changing it
changes the map's Edit Mode Size, so it goes the way Spell panel size does: written into
the layout, then the reload prompt. **Call:** no separate size row; square is one size.

### Size and spot (Square)

- Edit Mode Size 100% (stored Size 5; today 2 = 70%). The round map's frame is then 198 by
  198 (`M/Mainline/Minimap.xml:60`); with a 1-unit edge all round that is the mockup's 200.
- Centred on the screen's middle (today the round map is 4 right of it), its bottom edge 4
  up from the screen's bottom: the edge box is x -100 to 100, y 4 to 204 in the plan's
  units (1365.33 by 768). `HeaderUnderneath` stays 1, so the game's own offsets of the map
  inside its frame (`M/Camelot/Minimap.lua:68-76`) are the ones already measured; the
  builder derives `MAP.x` / `MAP.y` for Square from them and from the probe's 253 frame.
- The plan's built-in spots that change with Square, so that with the owner's present
  offsets the screen matches the mockup: the three swing timers 14 up; the LB and RB
  targeting pictures 20 outwards each (mirrored). Nothing else moves. The map stays one
  Arrange piece (`map`), now without a title strip rectangle.
- With the owner's arrangement this leaves, at the nearest points: 3 to the inner spell
  panels, 2 to the LB+LT / RB+RT prompts, 4 to the targeting pictures, 4 to the lowest
  swing timer. `Plan.Overlaps` must report 0 for Square with default offsets and with the
  owner's offsets from the 2026-11-25 log (map 0,-2; diamonds 0,-16; targeting pictures
  ±94,-56; bars ∓18,-16; buffs 0,4), at every Spell panel size. If a Spell panel size makes
  the inner panels reach past x ±100, the plan says so and the test names the size;
  **call:** the map is not shrunk to fit, the row's status line reports the overlap.

### The edge

The black disc (`PlaceDisc`) becomes, for Square, a black square of our own on the map's
parent, one frame level under the map, 1 screen unit wider than the map on each side, plus
a faint light line 1 unit inside the edge (white at 12%) on a frame of ours over the map
that takes no taps. Round keeps the disc.

### The words inside the map

On one frame of ours, parented to the map, above it, taking no taps:

- **Place name**, top middle, the game's zone colouring kept (the text and colour are read
  with the same calls the game's strip uses; the builder cites them from
  `M/Camelot/MinimapZoneTextButton.lua`), outlined, cut with "..." at the map's width.
- **Clock**, bottom right, the same time the game's strip shows (local or realm as the
  game's setting says), updated once a minute.
- **Coordinates**, bottom left. The game already draws these (`PlayerCoords`,
  `M/Mainline/Minimap.xml:287-294`). **Call:** move the game's own text into the corner if
  an engine-only `SetPoint` on that frame holds (it is not an Edit Mode system frame); if
  the game puts it back, hide it and draw our own, updated twice a second and only while
  the player moves.

The game's strip (`MinimapCluster.ZoneTextButton`, with the clock in it) is hidden while
Square is on, the way the two map buttons are hidden today, and shown again by Off. The
**Map buttons** row has nothing to show beside a hidden strip: with Square it is greyed
with the line "Round map only". Whatever a tap on the strip did (the builder reads
it from `M/Camelot/MinimapZoneTextButton.lua` and names it in the CHANGELOG) is not
available while Square is on.

Nothing here ticks while Square is off or the layout is off.

### The pieces the game hangs on the map's rim

Tracking, calendar, mail, the day/night mark, zoom buttons, the addon list button, the
instance difficulty flag (`M/Mainline/Minimap.xml`, `M/Camelot/*.xml`). They are placed
for a round rim. **Call:** this version does not move them. What is hidden today stays
hidden; the builder lists each remaining one with where it lands on the square, and the
snapshot gains a line for each, so one screenshot and one snapshot from the device decide
what to move in a follow-up.

### Quest icons

`QuestIcons.lua` keeps icons inside a circle and slides hand-in spots to the rim
(lines 847-867). With Square the limit is the square: an icon shows while both its x and y
distance are inside the half-width, and a hand-in spot beyond it is slid along its
direction to the square's edge. The shape is asked from the layout each time the pin list
is refreshed, not each tick. With the rotating-map setting on, the game turns the ground
inside the mask, not the mask, so the same rule holds.

### Off, and failing safely

- Off, or Controller layout off: the game's mask is given back, the strip shown, our edge
  and words hidden, Size back to 70%, the plan's old spots.
- If `SetMaskTexture` is missing or raises (all calls go through `pcall`): Square falls
  back to the round look at 100% with the disc, the debug log says once
  `map look: the square mask was refused`, and the row's status line says the same in
  plain words.
- All of it out of a fight only, through `PE:RunOutOfCombat`, as the ring is today.

### Where the code goes

`ControllerLayout.lua` is at Lua's limit of 200 top-level locals. The mask, the edge and
the words go in a new file, `addons/PocketEssentials/MapLook.lua` (`PE.MapLook`), called
from the places that place and restore the ring; the plan's numbers stay in `Plan`.

### Logging

Lines starting `map look:` — on/off and the size; the mask given, and given again after a
redraw (once per redraw, not repeated when nothing changed); the edge's rectangle; where
the coordinates text ended up and whether it is the game's or ours; each rim piece's
rectangle; the fallback line above. The snapshot lists the map's edge box and the words'
frame.

## Unverified until the device (to be listed in ARCHITECTURE)

| What | If it is wrong |
|---|---|
| `Minimap:SetMaskTexture("Interface\\Buttons\\WHITE8X8")` from addon code gives a square map, and the file exists on this client | The map stays round at 100% with a square edge behind it; the fallback draws the disc instead |
| Nothing but `UpdateMinimapConfig` gives the round mask back | The map turns round until the next placement; the log shows no "given again" line at that moment |
| The game's quest areas, the player arrow and the group dots draw correctly into the corners | Corners are empty or icons are cut at the old circle |
| An engine `SetPoint` on `PlayerCoords` holds | Our own coordinates text is used |
| The map at Edit Mode Size 100% is 198 with the frame offsets measured at 70% scaled up | The edge box is a few units off; the snapshot shows by how much |

## As built

Built on 2026-11-25 in four commits on `playing-screen-0.17.0` (the plan's Tasks 1 to 4).
Offline: `scripts/check.sh`, `scripts/test.sh` (1,100 feature tests, 62 of them new),
`scripts/audit-api.sh` and `scripts/build.sh` exit 0; the taint check lists 21 new `engine`
lines for `MapLook.lua` and no new kept call. Not yet tested on a device. Where the build
departs from the text above, and why:

1. **The map's low spot is not written into the Edit Mode layout.** The spec asks the builder
   to derive `MAP.y` so the map's bottom is 5 up. At 100% the map's bottom is `y + 30 +
   (253 - 198) / 2 = y + 57.5`, so that needs `y = -52.5`. Blizzard's source says the game
   will not take it: every Edit Mode piece is `clampedToScreen`
   (`Blizzard_EditMode/Shared/EditModeSystemTemplates.xml:4`) with the room of its own
   selection box (`.lua:174-185`, `.xml:113`), and the map's piece starts at the written spot,
   so a spot under the screen's edge is pushed back up to it. So the layout holds `x = -10,
   y = 0` (Size 5), and `MapLook.lua` lowers the map's frame, `MinimapCluster.MinimapContainer`,
   inside its piece with that frame's own engine `SetPoint` (it is no Edit Mode system; the
   game anchors it the same way in `SetHeaderUnderneath`, `M/Camelot/Minimap.lua:72-73`). The
   offset is worked out from the frame's real height, the map's height and the scale, so the
   map's bottom lands 5 up at any size. `SetHeaderUnderneath` is hooked (never called) so the
   frame is lowered again after Edit Mode anchors it. If neither offset in lines 70-73 scales
   with Size was the question: neither does (both are divided by the frame's scale, which the
   frame multiplies back). The same holding on the screen means a saved Arrange offset that
   moves the map below the bottom edge (the 2026-11-25 "map 0,-2") does nothing in the game,
   for the round map too; the plan's rectangle still shows it 2 lower.
2. **The clock is not in the strip.** It is a frame of its own, `TimeManagerClockButton`, a
   child of `MinimapCluster` anchored on the strip's right
   (`Blizzard_TimeManager/Mainline/Blizzard_TimeManager.xml:178`, Camelot overrides 5-7). Both
   are hidden. A tap on the strip opened the world map (`M/Mainline/Minimap.lua:110-112`); a
   tap on the clock the game's clock window (`Blizzard_TimeManager.lua:377-388`).
3. **The time is not read as `M/Mainline/GameTime.lua` does** but as
   `Blizzard_FrameXMLUtil/GameTimeUtil.lua:9-33, 77-93` does (that is where `GameTime_GetTime`
   lives). The same calls are made, the Lua function is not called. The settings are read with
   `C_CVar.GetCVar`, because `GetCVarBool` is a Lua function on this client
   (`Blizzard_SharedXMLBase/CvarUtil.lua:33`). The clock is read again every 20 seconds.
4. **0 overlaps does not hold for Square with Slim bars On and nothing moved.** The targeting
   pictures' 20 outwards exists so that pictures moved in beside the map (the 2026-11-25
   arrangement: 94 in, 56 down) end 4 short of the square instead of 16 inside it. At their
   built-in spot they then span 198 to 234 from the centre, and the slim bars begin at 218:
   three pairs overlap (LB picture with the player bar; RB picture with the target bar and
   with the room kept for the target's boxes), 16 by 14 at most. No single built-in spot
   clears both cases. Kept as the spec says, the exception is named in a test, and the
   Controller card says "With the square map 3 pairs of pieces overlap. Arrange the screen
   shows which, and moves them." With the game's own frames (Slim bars Off) and nothing
   moved, and with the 2026-11-25 arrangement at 80%, 90% and 100%, there is no overlap. That
   arrangement at 110% and 120% has its targeting pictures meet the larger inner panels, with
   the round map as with the square one.
5. **No Spell panel size brings a panel to the map** (`Plan.SQUARE_TIGHT_SIZES` is empty): the
   inner panels' inner edge is at 103 at every size, 3 clear of the edge box.
6. **The swing timers keep their place across** (centred on x 4, the round map's centre) and
   only rise 14, as the plan's test asks; they are not centred on the square map.
7. **"Map look" sits under "Slim bars"**, not beside "Map buttons": an existing test fixes
   which row is seventh. That test's list of row names gained the one new name. "Map buttons"
   is not greyed (the settings have no greyed choice row): its name reads "Map buttons: Round
   map only" and a tap is only remembered.
8. **The row counts at once in both directions.** Square to Round gives the mask, the strip,
   the words, the frame's place and the round map's disc back before the reload; Round to
   Square puts the square mask and the words on the still-small map. The targeting pictures
   follow at once too (they are placed live); the map's size and the timers follow the reload.
9. **The fallback keeps the square's layout.** When the mask is refused the map is round at
   100% with its disc, still lowered, the strip still hidden and the words still drawn (the
   clock and the coordinates then sit outside the circle). A mask the game accepts but does
   not draw cannot be told apart from one that worked.
10. **The coordinates:** the game's own text is used only while the game's
    `minimapShowPlayerCoords` setting is on (it is empty otherwise); it is then moved into the
    corner and raised over the map (`SetFrameLevel`), and checked at the next placement. In
    every other case the addon's own text is drawn, re-read twice a second and rewritten only
    when it reads differently.
11. **Pieces on the map's rim, listed and not moved** (debug lines `map look: rim:` and the
    snapshot): the day and night mark (`MinimapCluster.DielFrame`; at 100% it lands in the
    square's top right corner and reaches about 7 over its top edge, into the lowest swing
    timer's row), the mail mark (`MinimapCluster.IndicatorFrame`; over the hidden strip's left
    end, so inside the square's lower left, 31 to 55 up), the two zoom buttons (hidden until
    the map is pointed at; bottom right, by the clock), the event button, the group finder's
    button, the holder for the controller's buttons, the game's coordinates text and clock.
    The instance flag does not exist on this client.
12. **The stand-in.** `tests/lib/fakeclient.lua` gained `c:addMapParts` (the map's frame, the
    strip, the clock, the coordinates text, two rim pieces, and `MinimapCluster` guarded as an
    Edit Mode frame); the smoke test's clients call it. `tests/api/extract.lua` lists three
    more frame names.

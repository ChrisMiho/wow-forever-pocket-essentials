# Pocket Essentials

**The quality-of-life features you would normally piece together from half a dozen addons, in one small package built for playing WoW Forever on a handheld.**

Quest objectives on your map, an arrow to follow, one bag that sorts itself, vendor chores done for you, and chat you can use without hitting tiny targets. Everything works by touch and controller together, with big buttons and no typed commands.

![All in one, lightweight, made for handhelds](PILLARS_URL)

- **All in one:** one addon, one settings window, one switch per feature. Turn on what you want and leave the rest off.
- **Lightweight:** no libraries, and a feature that is switched off does no work at all.
- **Made for handhelds:** big buttons for touch and controller, no precise taps, no slash commands.

## Features

![Quest icons and arrow](QUESTS_URL)

- Icons on the **world map and minimap** for your quest objectives: kill, loot, use, explore and turn in, each with its own colour.
- Show icons for your **tracked quests**, only the **focused** one, or **all** of them.
- **Spots per objective** keeps busy quests readable (5 by default).
- Hides Blizzard's **blue haze** areas, so you see exactly where to go.
- An **arrow** to the nearest objective of the quest you focus in your quest log, with the distance. It tells you which zone to head to when the quest is elsewhere.
- An **Icon key** page in the settings explains every icon.

![Bag sorter](BAGS_URL)

- All your bags in **one combined window**.
- A **big Sort button**, and a stick click (**L3**, **R3** or none, you choose) sorts while the bag is open.
- Three sizes to fit your screen.

![Vendor chores, done](VENDOR_URL)

- **Sells your grey junk** automatically when you open a vendor.
- **Repairs your gear** from your own gold at vendors that can repair.
- Each has its own switch.

![Big chat buttons](CHAT_URL)

- Tap the round **PE** button for a picker with **one big button per channel** you can talk in (Say, Party, Guild, General, Trade and so on).
- Your **last six whisperers** as big name buttons, newest first.
- A **badge** on the button tells you when a whisper is waiting for an answer.

![One easy settings window](SETTINGS_URL)

- A big on/off switch for every feature, plus big buttons for each feature's choices.
- A **Reload UI** button, so you never need to type a reload.
- An **Errors** page that shows anything that went wrong in large, readable text.

## Getting started

1. Install Pocket Essentials. **QuestieDB** installs with it automatically.
2. Log in. A round **PE** button appears at the left edge of the screen.
3. Tap it to open the settings, and switch on the features you want. Every feature starts switched off.

Other ways to open the settings: hold the round button for half a second (always opens the settings, even when it is set to open chat), use the addon button on the minimap, or type `/pe`.

## Quest data from QuestieDB

The quest icons and the quest arrow use quest data from **[QuestieDB](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/questiedb)**, made by the Questie team. It is a required dependency, so CurseForge installs it for you. Everything else in Pocket Essentials works without it; if QuestieDB is missing, the two quest features stay off and the settings window tells you why.

## Early days

Pocket Essentials is young (version 0.0.x). It is developed on a Retroid Pocket 6, where the quest features have been tested in game; the other features are newer. It should work on any device that runs the WoW Forever client. If something goes wrong, the settings window's Errors page shows what happened. Bug reports and ideas are very welcome on [GitHub](https://github.com/ChrisMiho/wow-forever-pocket-essentials/issues).

# Pocket Essentials

**The stuff you miss from your desktop addons, rebuilt for thumbs. One small addon for playing WoW Forever on a handheld.**

Quest dots on your map, an arrow to follow, bags that sort with one press, greys that sell themselves, and chat you can use without poking at tiny tabs. Touch and controller both work everywhere. Nothing needs a typed command.

![Nine things, one addon, use what you want](OVERVIEW_URL)

Everything starts **off**. Flip on the bits you like. Whatever stays off does nothing at all, so your frames are safe. No libraries either.

## Stop guessing where the boars are

![Quest icons and the quest arrow](QUESTS_URL)

- **Quest icons:** dots on the world map and minimap for kill, loot, use, explore and turn in. One colour each.
- Show your tracked quests, just the focused one, or all of them.
- Busy quest? Cap the dots per objective, or merge the ones that sit on top of each other.
- Blizzard's blue quest haze is gone, so you see the actual spot.
- **Quest arrow:** points at the closest objective of your focused quest, with the distance. Quest in another zone? It tells you which.

## Messy bags? Sorted in one press.

![Bigger bag and Bag sorting](BAGS_URL)

- **Bag sorting:** press a stick in (left or right, you pick) and the bag is sorted. There is a big Sort tab on the side of the bag too.
- **Bigger bag:** three sizes, with your bags in one row along the top.
- The d-pad reaches the Sort tab too.

## Your thumb is not a mouse

![Chat picker](CHAT_URL)

- **Chat picker:** tap the round **PE** button and pick who to talk to: Say, Party, Guild, General, Trade and so on. One big button each.
- Your last six whispers, newest first.
- A red badge when someone is waiting on you.
- Chat text too small? There's a size control for that.

## The small stuff, taken care of

![Auto sell junk, Auto repair, Menu background and Loot sparkles](VENDOR_URL)

Four little helpers that work by themselves once they're switched on. Each has its own switch.

- **Auto sell junk:** open a vendor and your greys are sold. One line in chat says what went and for how much.
- **Auto repair:** fixes your gear out of your own gold.
- **Menu background:** puts a dark panel behind the game's pop-up menus so you can read them.
- **Loot sparkles:** keeps the sparkle on lootable things, even on the lowest graphics.

## Set it up once, then go play

![Setup guide and settings](SETUP_URL)

The first time you log in, a short guide walks you through each helper. Everything starts off, so you only switch on what you want. Touch works, and so does the d-pad with A and B.

After that it all lives in one settings window: a tab for each helper, a green dot on the ones that are on, big switches and no typing. Off means off, so nothing runs in the background. If something breaks, the Errors tab shows it in plain text.

Anything the addon puts on screen can be moved: **Move on screen** nudges the arrow, the "Press A to type" text and the PE button with the d-pad or big on-screen buttons, no dragging. The PE button comes in three sizes and in gold, Horde red or Alliance blue.

## Getting started

1. Install Pocket Essentials. **QuestieDB** installs with it automatically.
2. Log in. A round **PE** button appears at the left edge of the screen, and the setup guide opens.
3. Flip on what you want. Everything starts off.

Later, tap the round button to open the settings (hold it for half a second if the Chat picker opens instead), use the addon button on the minimap, or type `/pe`.

## Quest data from QuestieDB

The quest icons and the quest arrow use quest data from **[QuestieDB](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/questiedb)**, made by the Questie team. It is a required dependency, so CurseForge installs it for you. Everything else in Pocket Essentials works without it; if QuestieDB is missing, the two quest helpers stay off and the settings window tells you why.

## Tested on a handheld

Pocket Essentials is developed and tested on a Retroid Pocket 6. It should work on any device that runs the WoW Forever client. If something goes wrong, the Errors tab in the settings shows what happened. Bug reports and ideas are very welcome on [GitHub](https://github.com/ChrisMiho/wow-forever-pocket-essentials/issues).

The pictures on this page are illustrations drawn in the addon's own colours and wording, not screenshots. Real screenshots are in the gallery.
